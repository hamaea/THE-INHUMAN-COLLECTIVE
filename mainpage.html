Design
that’s meant
for mankind,
should be designed by
mankind.

We work within a system where output is constant, where form is generated, corrected, and repeated without pause, and where design is measured by speed, consistency, and scale.

We do not reject this system, but we question its authorship.

Design carries decisions, including pressure, hesitation, and adjustment, that cannot be automated without loss.

What is made for people should contain evidence of people. A surface can be complete and still lack origin. A result can be perfect and still be empty of touch.

We value what remains visible, the uneven edge, the slight shift, the unresolved form. These are conditions of being made. We do not correct them or remove them. We leave them.

What is made by hand cannot be fully replaced, only approximated. The system produces endlessly. We intervene deliberately, not to oppose, but to restore presence. The surface remains, but it is no longer final.

–

THE INHUMAN COLLECTIVE
